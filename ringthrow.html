About the Event

Ring Throw is a fun-filled skill game that tests your aim, focus, and hand–eye coordination. Players must toss rings accurately to land them on targets and score points. Simple to play yet challenging to master, this game brings excitement, quick competition, and lots of cheers from the crowd.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each participant gets a fixed number of rings and must throw from the marked line only.
  • Points are awarded only for rings that land correctly on the target; crossing the line invalidates the throw.
  • The highest score wins, and the event coordinator’s decision is final.
