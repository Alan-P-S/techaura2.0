About the Event

BALABALAM is a high-energy physical strength test designed to push participants to their limits. The event includes challenges like Arm wrestling,pull-ups, barbell or dumbbell holds, and hanging endurance, testing raw strength, stamina, and mental toughness. It’s all about power, control, and determination.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each participant competes individually.
  • Valid student ID is mandatory for entry.
  • Events include pull-ups, hanging endurance, weight hold (barbell/dumbbell), and Arm Wrestling.
  • Proper form and technique must be maintained during all challenges.
  • Any unsafe action or misuse of equipment will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Participants must strictly follow the instructions given by event coordinators and judges.
  • Judges’ decisions will be final and binding.
  • Safety Disclaimer: Participants are responsible for their own physical fitness and health. The organizers will not be held liable for any injury, strain, or health issues occurring during the event. Participants are advised to perform within their limits and follow all safety instructions provided by the coordinators.
