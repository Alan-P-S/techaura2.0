About the Event

The IEDC and ISTE of Maharajas Technological Institute, Thrissur, are jointly organizing a Hackathon based on UN Sustainable Development Goal 9 (SDG-9): This year is all about SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure , using smart engineering to build a sustainable future

Problem Statement

To design innovative, affordable, and scalable technology solutions that strengthen industry, innovation and infrastructure

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team must consist of 3–4(max) students.
  • Teams will be given the Exact theme for their Presentation on 2 hours prior (16/1/2026 Morning 9 AM)
  • Teams will be presenting their solutions/ideas by 11AM to the judges.
  • The idea must be original; plagiarism will lead to disqualification.
  • Solutions may be hardware, software, simulation, or system design.
  • All development must be done during the hackathon duration.
  • Judges decision will be final and binding.
  • Discipline, teamwork, and ethical conduct are mandatory throughout the event.
  • Teams may use PPT's or any other methods for their Presentation
  • All teams must bring their own laptops and required accessories
