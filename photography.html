About the Event

The Photography Competition celebrates creativity and storytelling through images, giving participants a platform to capture powerful moments, unique perspectives, and emotions using their photographic skills.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Only mobile phones are allowed for photography.
  • DSLR, mirrorless cameras, tablets, or external lenses are not permitted.
  • Single participant entry only (no teams).
  • The theme will be announced on the spot and must be strictly followed.
  • Participants must capture the photo only within the event area and within the allotted time
  • Only one photograph is allowed per participant. Basic editing is allowed, such as: Cropping Brightness / Contrast Color correction
  • No heavy manipulation, filters, AI enhancements, or object removal.
  • The photo must be: Original Captured during the contest Not previously taken or reused
  • Metadata (EXIF data) may be checked to verify authenticity. Photographs containing offensive, inappropriate, or copyrighted content will be disqualified.
  • Participants must submit the photo before the deadline via the method announced (QR / WhatsApp / Google Form).
  • The decision of the judges will be final and binding.
